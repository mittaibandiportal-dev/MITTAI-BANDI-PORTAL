Need Help With an Order?

Fastest Resolution

For the fastest support with an in-progress order, please use call or chat support inside the MITTAI BANDI app. Navigate to “My Orders”, select your active order, and tap the help option.

For refunds, missing items, or order-related complaints after delivery, write to order@mittaibandi.com. Please include your order number and clear photos of the items. You will receive an official ticket number to track your resolution.

Tip: In-app chat connects you directly with our real-time dispatch and delivery partners.

Want to List Your Sweet Shop?

Vendor Onboarding

If you own a sweet shop, bakery, or confectionery and want to expand your reach with MITTAI BANDI, email us at info@mittaibandi.com with your shop name, location, contact number, and FSSAI license number.

Our vendor onboarding team will review your details and guide you through storefront setup, packaging standards, and digital order management.
