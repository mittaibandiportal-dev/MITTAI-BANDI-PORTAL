Mittai Bandi – Contact Us

We're here to help. Whether you have a question about an order, a refund, food safety, a partnership or anything else, please reach out through the channel below that fits best.

Privacy Policy Terms of Service Cancellation & Refund API Policy Contact Us

Need Help With an Order?

For the fastest help with an order that is in progress, use call or chat support in the Mittai Bandi app. Go to “My Orders”, select your order, and tap the help option.

For refunds, missing or wrong items, or any order-related complaint after delivery, write to order@mittaibandi.com. Please include your order number, and clear photos of the items if there is a problem with them. You will receive a ticket number so you can track your complaint.

Want to List Your Sweet Shop?

If you own a sweet shop, bakery or confectionery and want to reach more customers on Mittai Bandi, write to info@mittaibandi.com with your shop name, location, contact number and FSSAI license number. Our team will get back to you with the next steps. You can also submit an enquiry on our Partner With Us page.

Food Safety and Hygiene

Your health matters to us. If you think a product made you unwell, or you want to report a hygiene concern about a Partner Shop, please write to hygiene@mittaibandi.com with your order number and details. If you need medical attention, please seek it first.

Not Satisfied? Escalate Your Complaint

  1. Step 1: Use call or chat support in the Mittai Bandi app.
  2. Step 2: Write to order@mittaibandi.com and note your ticket number.
  3. Step 3: If still unresolved, call +91-9494614551 (Monday to Friday, 9:00 am – 6:00 pm) and quote your ticket number. We aim to address escalated concerns within an estimated 48 hours.
  4. Further escalation: You can contact our Grievance Officer using the details below.
