Regional Heritage

India's Sweet Geography.

From the ghee-soaked sweets of Andhra Pradesh to the chhena delicacies of Bengal — India's confectionery tradition is as diverse as its culture.

🏛️

Andhra & Telangana

Ariselu, Pootharekulu, Bobbatlu and the famous Tirupati Laddu — rich traditions of Telugu confectionery.

🌺

Bengal & East India

Rasgulla, Sandesh, Mishti Doi, Chum Chum — the delicate art of chhena-based Bengali sweets.

🪔

Rajasthan & North India

Ghevar, Churma Laddu, Kalakand, Mawa Kachori — the celebratory sweets of the royal kitchens.

🥥

South India

Mysore Pak, Rava Laddu, Kajjaya, Soan Papdi variations — the rich, ghee-laden traditions of southern India.
