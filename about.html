Our Story

Bridging Tradition
and Technology.

Food is more than sustenance. It is memory, celebration and culture.

Across India, traditional sweets and snacks hold a special place in every home, festival and milestone. Yet accessing authentic, high-quality delicacies from trusted local confectioners is not always simple.

MITTAI BANDI was conceptualized to address this gap — bringing together local vendors, traditional artisans and consumers under one unified digital storefront.

From regional specialties and ghee-based sweets to crunchy savouries and festive gift boxes, MITTAI BANDI makes traditional culinary experiences accessible through technology.

Our Vision

Preserving Tradition
Through Technology.

India's confectionery tradition is vast, diverse and deeply personal. Behind every sweet shop is a story of craftsmanship, recipes passed down across generations and dedication to taste.

MITTAI BANDI believes modern technology should not replace tradition — it should preserve and elevate it. By providing traditional confectioners with digital infrastructure, we aim to help keep cherished recipes alive and thriving for future generations.

Our Mission

Three Pillars,
One Purpose.

Everything we do is built around three core commitments.

01

Empower Local Artisans

Give traditional sweet makers a platform to showcase their craftsmanship and reach a wider audience through digital infrastructure.

02

Improve Customer Convenience

Make discovering and ordering authentic traditional sweets and savouries simpler, more accessible and more enjoyable for everyone.

03

Celebrate Culinary Heritage

Preserve and promote India's rich regional food traditions, ensuring centuries-old recipes continue to thrive and reach new generations.
